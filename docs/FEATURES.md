# CounselPro — Feature Catalog

A comprehensive breakdown of the core modules and functional capabilities engineered in **CounselPro**.

---

## 🤖 1. AI Legal Assistant
- **24/7 Contextual Legal Q&A**: Answers preliminary queries on statutory definitions, court procedures, and legal terms.
- **Contract OCR & Analysis**: Scans uploaded legal agreements, extracts clauses, and identifies potential liabilities or ambiguous wording.
- **Statutory Citation Referencing**: Quickly points users to relevant legal codes, precedent summaries, and filing guidelines prior to advocate engagement.

---

## 🔍 2. Verified Advocate Directory & Smart Search
- **Bar Council Verification**: Every listed advocate is verified with their official Bar Council enrollment credentials.
- **Multi-Dimensional Filters**: Search by:
  - Specialization (Corporate, Criminal, Property, Family, Tax, Cyber Law, Civil Litigation)
  - Jurisdiction & Court (Supreme Court, High Courts, District & Sessions Courts)
  - Experience, Win Rates, and Client Ratings
  - Spoken Languages & Consultation Mode
- **Geospatial Map Discovery**: View advocates located near court complexes or within specific municipal jurisdictions.

---

## ⚖️ 3. Case Lifecycle & Milestone Tracking
- **Standardized 4-Stage Progression**:
  1. **Filed**: Initial petition, plaint, or advisory memo logged with court filing numbers.
  2. **Review**: Evidence preparation, advocate counter-filings, and documentation audit.
  3. **Hearing Scheduled**: Synchronized hearing dates, cause list tracking, and reminder alerts.
  4. **Resolved**: Final judgment, decree, or settlement documented.
- **Cause List & Timeline Sync**: Push notifications for upcoming court dates and filing deadlines.

---

## 🔒 4. Encrypted Case Vault & Secure Viewer
- **Client-Isolated Storage**: Each matter operates inside a dedicated cryptographic partition.
- **Multi-Format Support**: Secure preview of court orders, pleadings, vakalatnamas, and evidence PDFs.
- **Watermarking & Tamper Evidence**: Document access logging prevents unauthorized redistribution.

---

## 🚨 5. Emergency Legal SOS Connect
- **1-Touch Immediate Response**: Geared for urgent legal predicaments (bail, unlawful detention, roadside assistance).
- **On-Duty Advocate Pool**: Automatically notifies available advocates registered for emergency response within the local jurisdiction.
- **Live Location Sharing**: Transmits emergency coordinates to the responding legal counsel securely.

---

## 📞 6. Encrypted Communication Hub
- **Confidential Consultation Modes**:
  - High-definition secure video consultation
  - Crystal-clear encrypted voice calling
  - Structured real-time messaging with file attachment capabilities
- **Scheduled Consultations**: Integrated calendar slots with automated meeting invites.

---

## 💳 7. Retainer & Escrow Wallet
- **Milestone-Based Escrow**: Funds are safely held until predefined legal milestones or consultations are fulfilled.
- **Transparent Hourly Billing**: Clear logging of consultation duration with zero hidden surcharges.
- **Flexible Subscription Tiers**: Tiered memberships (`Free`, `Basic`, `Premium`, `VIP`) for regular corporate and individual legal advisory.

---

## 💼 8. Advocate Practice Workspace
- **Availability Management**: Set recurring calendar slots, consultation fees, and emergency SOS on-duty status.
- **Client Request Management**: Accept, reschedule, or review incoming client matters with brief summaries.
- **Practice Analytics & Earnings**: Visual performance indicators for cases handled, consultation revenue, and client satisfaction scores.

---

## 🛡️ 9. Administrative Governance & Compliance
- **KYC Verification Queue**: Dedicated administrative workflow to review advocate identity documents and Bar licenses.
- **Dispute Resolution & Audit**: Tools to investigate client reports, fee disagreements, or conduct reviews to ensure integrity.
