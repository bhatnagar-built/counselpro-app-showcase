# CounselPro — User Workflows

This document outlines the standard end-to-end operational workflows supported within **CounselPro**.

---

## 🧭 Flow 1: Client Legal Consultation & Matter Initiation

```
[Client Onboarding / Login]
         │
         ▼
[Explore & Search Advocates] ──► (Filter by Court, Specialization, Language)
         │
         ▼
[Select Advocate & Review Credentials]
         │
         ▼
[Schedule Consultation Slot] ──► (Video, Audio, or In-Person)
         │
         ▼
[Deposit Fee into Escrow Wallet]
         │
         ▼
[Attend Encrypted Consultation]
         │
         ▼
[Formalize Case Matter in Vault] ──► (Upload evidence & pleadings)
```

1. **Discovery**: Client filters advocates by court jurisdiction (e.g., Delhi High Court) and practice area (e.g., Corporate Arbitration).
2. **Engagement**: Reviews advocate ratings, Bar Council verification badge, and fee rates before picking an available calendar slot.
3. **Escrow Guarantee**: Retainer is deposited safely into the platform escrow wallet.
4. **Matter Formation**: Upon successful consultation, the matter is registered as an active case with dedicated vault storage.

---

## 🚨 Flow 2: Urgent Emergency Legal SOS

```
[Emergency Occurs]
         │
         ▼
[Press Central SOS Button] (1-Tap Activation)
         │
         ▼
[System Dispatches Geolocation & Incident Context]
         │
         ▼
[Closest Verified On-Duty Advocate Accepts]
         │
         ▼
[Immediate High-Priority Voice / Video Link Established]
         │
         ▼
[Intervention & Guidance Delivered (e.g., Police Station Bail)]
```

1. **One-Tap Activation**: Client triggers the emergency button directly from the bottom navigation bar.
2. **Geofenced Notification**: Alerts are sent to certified criminal and civil advocates on active standby within the immediate vicinity.
3. **Real-Time Intervention**: Advocate receives critical situational details and establishes immediate direct communication.

---

## 💼 Flow 3: Advocate Matter Management & Case Progression

```
[Advocate Dashboard]
         │
         ▼
[Access Active Case Workspace]
         │
         ▼
[Review Uploaded Pleadings & Evidence in Vault]
         │
         ▼
[Update Matter Stage] ──► (Filed ➔ Review ➔ Hearing Scheduled ➔ Resolved)
         │
         ▼
[Client Automatically Notified of Hearing Date & Next Steps]
         │
         ▼
[Milestone Completed & Retainer Released]
```

1. **Case Access**: Advocate accesses case files within the isolated encrypted vault without relying on unencrypted third-party channels.
2. **Milestone Tracking**: Updates court filing dates, case stage status, and hearing notes.
3. **Automated Synchronization**: Both client and legal team receive synchronized calendar reminders for upcoming cause list hearings.

---

## 🛡️ Flow 4: Advocate KYC Verification (Admin Governance)

```
[Advocate Submits Bar Council Registration & Government ID]
         │
         ▼
[Admin Receives Application in Verification Queue]
         │
         ▼
[Cross-Reference with Bar Council Database & Disciplinary Records]
         │
         ├─► Approved: "Verified Advocate" Badge Issued & Profile Published
         │
         └─► Rejected: Reason logged and applicant notified for re-submission
```
