# CounselPro — Product Roadmap

This roadmap highlights strategic enhancements and feature evolutions planned for **CounselPro**.

---

## 📍 Near-Term (Current Focus)
- [x] **Verified Advocate Matching Engine**: Multidimensional filtering by court jurisdiction, specialization, language, and pricing.
- [x] **AI Legal Assistant V1**: Contract OCR extraction, statutory citation summaries, and preliminary legal definitions.
- [x] **4-Stage Case Lifecycle Tracker**: Standardized milestones (`Filed` ➔ `Review` ➔ `Hearing` ➔ `Resolved`).
- [x] **Encrypted Case Vault & In-App Viewer**: Sandboxed viewing of pleadings, orders, and contracts.
- [x] **Emergency Legal SOS Connect**: Rapid geographical dispatch to certified on-duty legal counsel.
- [x] **Retainer & Milestone Escrow Wallet**: Transparent financial clearing and subscription tiers.

---

## 🔮 Medium-Term Initiatives
- [ ] **E-Courts & Cause List Automated Sync**: Real-time push notifications when a matter appears on daily court cause lists.
- [ ] **Interactive Multi-Party Video Hearing Rooms**: Secure, HD virtual consultation rooms with simultaneous document co-annotation.
- [ ] **Multi-Language Regional NLP**: Expansion of the AI Legal Assistant into regional court languages (Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Bengali).
- [ ] **Offline-First Vault Synchronization**: Encrypted on-device caching for courtrooms with limited cellular connectivity.

---

## 🚀 Long-Term Vision
- [ ] **Multi-Seat Law Firm Workspaces**: Enterprise collaboration tools for senior partners, associates, and paralegals.
- [ ] **Statutory Precedent Citation Graph**: Graph-based visualization of cited case law and active judgment relationships.
- [ ] **Direct Bar Council SSO & Dynamic License Status**: Real-time validation of advocate standing via automated verification endpoints.
